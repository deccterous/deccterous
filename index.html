Who We Are

About Us

DECCTEROUS - IC Design Club of ECE department, NIE Mysuru aims to provide practical knowledge as well as strong fundamentals in Electronics and Communication Engineering.

Our mission is to prepare students for a career in the Core Domains by equipping them with cutting-edge skills and industry knowledge. Through hands-on projects, industry interactions, and collaborative initiatives, we strive to nurture technical competence and innovation among our members.

Our Mission

The mission of the Circuit Design Club is to create an environment for experiential learning where every member gets an opportunity to acquire and develop the skills needed for pursuing a career in any Electronics and Communication domain.

🔭

Our Vision

To become an active centre of excellence in both analog and digital circuit design for various applications that can cater to the needs of the booming industry by bridging the gap between industry and academics.

📌

Our Objectives

The envisioned Integrated Circuit Design Club aims to:

  • Provide a forum for students and faculty members to engage in interactive discussions, workshops, and knowledge-sharing sessions related to circuit design principles, methodologies, and emerging technologies.
  • Build strong fundamentals in IC design that enable students to secure placements in core electronics companies.
  • Foster a collaborative environment that encourages hands-on projects, design competitions, and practical applications of circuit design theories.
  • Cultivate a network of students, researchers, and industry professionals to facilitate mentorship, career guidance, and collaboration opportunities.
  • Enhance the visibility and reputation of the department through participation in circuit design competitions, industry partnerships, and knowledge dissemination.

This club aligns with our commitment to providing students with practical and real-world knowledge to complement their academic learning.

Experiential Learning Activity

Open Book Test on Linear Integrated Circuits

To enhance conceptual understanding and problem-solving skills, we conducted an open book test on Linear Integrated Circuits (LIC) for our fourth-semester students. Rather than testing memorization, this approach encouraged critical thinking, application-based learning, and real-world problem-solving.

Topics Covered:

  • Differential Amplifier - CMOS design
  • Current Mirrors
  • Op-amp circuits - Linear, non-linear, application circuits

Why Open Book Test?

  • Encourages focus on understanding concepts
  • Promotes ability to analyze and apply knowledge
  • Develops research and referencing skills
